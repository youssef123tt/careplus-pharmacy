## Scope

CarePlus storefront (home/listing, product details, cart) and admin, web. Visitor mode: Operate (shopper completes a purchase decision; manager maintains the catalog). Light and dark themes required. Code-led.

## Constraints

Preserve all accessible names, test ids and copy the unit and e2e suites rely on. English only. No invented claims (delivery, licences, reviews).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every product is the face of its medicine carton: a printed category band, the name set like box copy, and the pack choices as strength chips on the side panel. Refuses the gradient hero + soft rounded card grid of every AI storefront.

OWN-WORLD: Cool carton-grey ground, white board surfaces with 1px board edges and 3px corners; ink near-black; one pharmacy green for actions only; category bands in flat full-strength hues (vitamins orange, personal care blue, first aid red, more from a fixed set). Barlow Condensed caps for product names and numerals, Barlow for UI. Dashed perforation rule separates face from side panel. Dark theme is the same carton printed on dark board, bands unchanged.

STORY: Shopper sees categories as coloured tabs, scans carton faces, picks a strength chip (1 / 4 / 12 packs) with its total in KWD, adds to cart, and trusts the totals and pack counts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Board header strip (CAREPLUS wordmark with green cross, Shop/Admin, theme toggle, cart count). Short condensed-caps title with one line on bundle pricing. Category tabs underlined in band colours, brand chips and search on one toolbar. Three-up carton cards: band, condensed name + illustration, spec line, perforation, strength chips, green add button.
